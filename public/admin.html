AUTO-BUYER

OFF
Picks a coin off the live feed and buys it, then holds until take profit or stop loss. The market cap floor is checked ON-CHAIN at buy time, so a coin that launched fine and died ten seconds later is skipped — the feed's own market cap is the price at creation and tells you nothing. Every curve opens at the same price in SOL, so the launch market cap in dollars follows SOL/USD (about $2,550 at SOL $91, about $4,200 at SOL $150): a floor just above launch means "not dumped", higher demands real buying, and a floor set for a different SOL price rejects everything. If nothing passes, the log says what the best coin on the feed was worth. P&L is measured as sellback value against what was paid, fees and exit impact included, so +70% means +70% in the wallet. Dry run does all of it on paper, including the exits. The daily cap resets at UTC midnight; the reserve is SOL the buyer never dips into so fees always clear.
